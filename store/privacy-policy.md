# Privacy policy: UK Pronunciation Coach

Last updated: 27 October 2026

UK Pronunciation Coach works entirely on your phone.

- **Recordings.** The microphone records you reading aloud so the app can score you.
  Audio is processed on the phone and is never uploaded. You can delete recordings and
  all other data in Settings.
- **Progress.** Scores, streaks and settings are stored on the phone only.
- **No tracking.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash
  reporting. We receive no data about you.
- **Network use.** The app makes no network requests while you practise. The only
  exception is the optional download of the larger speech model, which asks for
  confirmation, uses Wi-Fi by default, and sends no personal data.
- **Feedback.** "Send feedback" opens a draft in your own email app. You decide whether
  to attach your exported progress file and whether to send it. If you send it, we use
  it only to improve the app and delete it afterwards.
