# Synthetic eval set: sources and licences

Dev-side only. Nothing listed here ships in the app or is committed to git: model and
voice files go to the git-ignored `tools/eval/data/voices/`, audio to
`tools/eval/data/synth/`. The native VCTK set and Britfone are covered in
`tools/eval/evalset/SOURCES.md`.

Checked at the original sources on 2026-10-20.

Rejected or not used:

- Every other Piper en_GB voice: fine-tuned from `lessac` (Blizzard 2013, research only) or
  `ryan` (CC BY-NC-SA), or trained on non-commercial data. See C4's table in
  `docs/licences.md`.
- The `kokoro` / `misaki` text front-ends and the Piper runtime (`piper1-gpl`, GPL-3.0):
  not needed. Both engines read phonemes that `tools/eval/synth/phonemes.py` writes, and
  they run on onnxruntime (MIT). eSpeak NG is not needed for synthesis either.

SHA-256 of every file is pinned in `tools/eval/synth/voices.py` (`FILES`) and checked by
`python -m tools.eval.synth.build fetch`.

## Noise and reverb

No recorded noise or impulse responses. White, pink and brown noise and the room
responses (exponentially decaying noise) are generated by numpy with a per-clip seed in
`tools/eval/synth/augment.py`, so there is no third-party audio to license.

## Sentences

`tools/eval/synth/sentences.tsv` (committed):

- 200 sentences from the app's own passages (`assets/content/passages.v1.json`, C1, written
  for the app), 4-12 words, spread over the 6 levels. Expected phonemes come from the bundle
  itself (Britfone 3.0.1, MIT, with C1's fixes).
- 100 VCTK sentence texts (CC-BY-4.0, "CSTR VCTK Corpus (University of Edinburgh)"), the
  ones the native set already uses, 4-12 words. Expected phonemes from Britfone 3.0.1 (MIT).
