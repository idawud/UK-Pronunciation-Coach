=== l3-001 | A walk in the park
focus: ɑː ɔː
Last March, Martha took her dog for a long walk in the park. The grass was wet and the sky was dark, but they walked for hours. They passed a small farm, a stone bridge and a garden full of roses. When they got home, Martha ran a warm bath and made a large pot of tea.

=== l3-002 | Email to a landlord
focus: ɔː ɑː
Dear Mark, I'm writing about the flat on Church Road. The heating in the bedroom has stopped working, and the bathroom door won't close properly. Could you send someone to look at them before the weekend? I'm usually at home after four o'clock. I'd be grateful for a quick answer. Many thanks, Laura.

focus: ɑː ɜː

=== l3-005 | The market

My daughter always wants to stop at the flower stall, and she normally talks me into buying a bunch of daffodils for our hall.

=== l3-006 | First driving lesson

I should have called you earlier, and I apologise for that. Are you free on Thursday or Friday afternoon instead? I can come to your office at any time. Best wishes, Sean.

=== l3-008 | The surprise party
focus: ɑː ɜː

She laughed so hard that she nearly cried. Later, we danced on the lawn until after midnight, and nobody wanted to go home.

=== l3-009 | Caught in the storm
focus: ɔː ɑː
We were halfway up the mountain when the storm started. The wind roared, the rain poured down and the path turned to mud. We sheltered behind a large rock and waited. After an hour, the sky cleared, and we saw the whole valley below us. It was worth the walk, but we were soaked.

=== l3-012 | Lost at the airport
focus: ɔː ɜː
When I landed in Bristol, my suitcase didn't arrive. I waited at the carousel for nearly an hour, but it never appeared. Finally, I reported it at the desk, and a kind woman wrote down my address. Two days later, a courier brought it to my door. Nothing was missing, though my shirts were badly creased.

=== l3-019 | A day at the seaside
focus: ɑː iː

=== l3-020 | The broken heater
focus: ɔː ɑː
It was the coldest morning of the year, and the heater in our office broke. By nine o'clock, everyone was wearing coats, scarves and gloves at their desks. Our manager ordered a large pot of soup for lunch and promised that the engineer would arrive before four. He finally turned up at half past, and we all cheered.
