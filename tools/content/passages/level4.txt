=== l4-001 | New library opens
focus: ə
A new library has opened in the centre of Fairfield after two years of building work. The council spent four million pounds on the project, which includes a children's area, a cafe and twenty computers for the public. Local residents were invited to the opening on Saturday morning, and hundreds of families came to explore the shelves. The head librarian said that the building was designed to be a place for everyone, not just for readers. It will be open from nine until eight on weekdays, and until five at the weekend.

=== l4-011 | Bus fares rise
focus: ə eə
Bus fares across the region will rise by ten per cent from the first of April, the transport authority announced yesterday. A single journey in the city centre will cost two pounds twenty, compared with two pounds today. The authority said the increase was necessary because of rising fuel and staff costs. However, passengers' groups have criticised the decision, arguing that higher fares will push more people into their cars. Students and pensioners will continue to receive discounts, and weekly tickets will still be available at the current price until June.

=== l4-015 | Hospital car park
focus: ə ɑː

Staff will also be able to park for free, although spaces remain limited at busy times.

=== l4-020 | Recycling changes
focus: ə
From the beginning of next month, the council is changing the way it collects household waste. Food waste will be collected every week in a small brown bin, while general rubbish will be collected every two weeks instead of weekly. Glass, paper and plastic can still be placed in the green recycling bin. Every home will receive a new bin and a leaflet explaining the changes. The council says the new system will save money and help the environment, but some residents are worried that bins will overflow in summer.
